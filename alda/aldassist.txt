# Executive Summary

## What ALDASSIST is

**[F]** ALDASSIST is a patent ecosystem for inventors, patent professionals, law firms and innovation teams, positioned as **"the operating layer between an invention and the patent system"** (Phase 2 §4). It spans three layers: **the Record** (custody — disclosure capture, vault, docket, deadline engine, portfolio), **the Judgement** (AI assessment with human verification, cost modelling, abandon/renew), and **the Work** (drafting, filing, prosecution, renewals delivered by verified agents). Competitors own one layer; the repository's thesis is that **the integration is the product**.

**[F]** The strategic thesis is that patent services are a *lemons market* — buyers cannot evaluate quality at purchase or for 3–5 years afterwards — and such markets are won not by being better but by being **first to make quality visible**, through accumulated outcome metadata that cannot be bought (Phase 2 §6.2, §16.2).

**[F]** MVP scope is India + PCT, patents only, proving exactly one hypothesis: *that an inventor will trust an AI-assisted, human-verified assessment enough to pay for the filing that follows* (Phase 2 §13.1).

## Current maturity

**[F]** The repository contains ~460 KB of documentation across 33 tracked files. Planning is complete and frozen through **Phase 5 (User Flows)**. Phases 6–9 have not started. `03_Design` and `04_Development` contain README stubs only — **no design assets, no code, no schema, no API specification, no infrastructure plan**.

**[F]** Governance is unusually mature for a pre-implementation repository: a canonical principles document (AP-01…AP-14), a canonical glossary (20 structured terms), a canonical metrics document (8 defined metrics), a canonical status source, a 16-entry Decision Log on a standard template, an approved 15-decision governance Decision Register, an independent audit, and 13 historical notices preserving frozen documents without rewriting them.

## Overall assessment

**[I]** This is a **strategy- and architecture-complete, implementation-empty** repository. The thinking is substantially deeper than typical pre-seed documentation — Phase 3 alone specifies 8 architectural principles, 30 modules, 20 business rules, a 4-tier AI boundary model, a three-zone data architecture, and a rules-as-data deadline engine. The gap is not quality of thought; it is that **nothing has been converted into technical artifacts**.

# 16. Final Product Summary

**ALDASSIST is the operating layer between an invention and the patent system.** It is not a filing service, a search engine, or an IP management system — it is the permanent record of what someone invented, the honest judgement of whether it is worth protecting, and the verified professional work to protect it, sustained across a patent's twenty-year life with every AI conclusion traceable to its source.

It serves two audiences as co-equals: **deep-tech startups and technology SMEs**, who have budget and urgency but cannot evaluate patent quality; and **registered patent agents**, whose capacity is the business's hard legal ceiling and who receive purpose-built tooling before they receive any client work. Individual inventors, students and researchers are served free, permanently and deliberately, as a trust and distribution investment.

The problem is structural: patent services are a lemons market where buyers cannot judge quality at purchase or for years afterwards, so price becomes the only visible signal and quality gets bid down. Simultaneously, AI has made expert analysis cheap — but every incumbent has aimed it at organisations that already have IP departments, leaving the front of the funnel, where inventions are actually lost, unserved.

ALDASSIST's difference is enforced honesty: provenance as a type constraint rather than a prompt instruction, mandatory named human review before any verdict reaches a client, "don't file" designed as carefully as "file", and a twenty-year cost shown before the filing fee. Its moat is outcome metadata — objection patterns and allowance rates by agent, examiner and art unit — which cannot be bought or accelerated with capital.

**Maturity:** strategy, architecture, information architecture and user flows are complete and frozen; governance is audited and version-controlled; design, technical architecture and code do not yet exist. **Long-term:** the system of record for the world's inventions — custody first, judgement second, liquidity last.
