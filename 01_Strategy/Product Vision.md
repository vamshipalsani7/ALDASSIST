## Mission

> To make the protection of an invention as reliable, transparent and affordable as sending money — so that the value of what people invent is determined by the quality of the idea, not the depth of the pocket or the luck of finding a good agent.

*(Phase 2 §1)*

The reasoning recorded with it: the patent system today produces radically different outcomes for two inventors with identical inventions, depending on who drafted their claims, whether they searched before disclosing, whether they understood renewal costs, and whether anyone told them the truth about their odds. That variance is the enemy. Everything built should reduce it.

Phase 2 §1 explicitly rejects "democratize IP" as a mission statement, on the grounds that it means nothing and is used by every competitor in the Indian market.

## Ten-Year Vision

> The system of record for the world's inventions — the place where an idea is first written down, assessed, protected, tracked, defended and eventually monetized, with every step traceable to its evidence.

*(Phase 2 §2)*

Three things must be true for this to have happened:

| # | Condition | Recorded assessment |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Custody.** A meaningful share of the world's active patent portfolios have their deadlines, documents and decision history on the platform — not because it is cheapest, but because moving off is dangerous. | Required |
| **2** | **Judgement.** When someone asks "is this worth protecting, and what will it cost me over twenty years," the platform's answer is the one they trust — including when the answer is no. | Required |
| **3** | **Liquidity.** Patents on the platform find licensees, buyers and partners more often than patents that don't. | "The least certain of the three" |

Phase 2 §2 records that conditions 1 and 2 produce "a large, durable, profitable company," and that condition 3 is what would make it "a very large one."

## Product Positioning

**Category.** Not a patent filing service, a search engine, an IP management system, or an AI tool. Those categories are recorded as occupied and commoditizing.

> **The operating layer between an invention and the patent system.**

*(Phase 2 §4)*

### The three layers

| Layer | What it is | Business role |
|---|---|---|
| **The Record** | Disclosure capture, document vault, docket, deadline engine, portfolio view | Custody. The switching cost. |
| **The Judgement** | AI assessment with human verification — novelty, patentability, cost modelling, abandon/renew decisions, FTO | Differentiation. The trust engine. |
| **The Work** | Drafting, filing, prosecution, renewals, delivered by verified agents | Revenue and margin. |

Competitors own one layer. **The integration is the product.** *(Phase 2 §4)*

## What the Product Is Not

Recorded in Phase 2 §4, and stated openly rather than left implicit:

- Not a law firm
- Not a replacement for a patent attorney
- Not a patent search engine competing with Google Patents
- Not a landscape or analytics tool
- Not an IP management system competing at enterprise scale — yet

## Geographic Vision

> India-first for product, users, supply and cost base. Global for revenue.

*(Phase 2 §12)*

Phase 2 §12.3 records the reasoning as load-bearing: India is "the beachhead and the factory. It is not the market." The recorded consequence is that product decisions differ from day one — a jurisdiction-agnostic data model, currency-agnostic pricing, jurisdiction-abstracted deadline rules, and English-first with Indian-language layers rather than the reverse.

Jurisdiction sequencing *(Phase 2 §12.4)*:

| Stage | Jurisdictions |
|---|---|
| MVP | India + PCT |
| V2 | + United States |
| V3 | + EPO / selected EP states |
| V4 | + China, Japan, Korea, GCC |
| Later | Everything else via agent network |

## Long-Term Extensibility

Phase 3 §23 records extensions the architecture must accommodate without rework — each *anticipated*, not built: new jurisdictions · other IP types · captive patent practice · licensing marketplace · enterprise deployment · trade secrets pathway · predictive prosecution models · public API · vernacular product · defensive publication.

The two decisions recorded as most protecting future optionality are **P5** (Invention as root, not Application) and **D1** (rules as data).
